# Brief: Instagram carousel, "The Drum That Listened"

**Requested:** Carousel under 10 pages for Instagram. Posted on Ekatvam's channel as a collaboration with Rajmohan Krishnan. Uses the supplied portrait of Padma Shri Guruvayur Dorai.
**Supplied:** tribute newsletter (`supplied_copy/`), portrait photograph, Ekatvam logo and palette.
**Format:** 9 slides, 1080 x 1350 px (4:5). Side margin 84 px, inside the grid-crop safe area.

VISUAL IDEA: The tribute is read like a quiet printed programme: the portrait opens it, one close detail of the hand on the drum carries the sound, and every other page is silence, a single sentence, or a ledger of names.

## Sequence
1. Cover: portrait fading into Oxblood, title, dates
2. "Today the mridangam lost one of its last links to a golden age." with one logo brushstroke
3. Beginnings: ledger of 1935 to 1953
4. Detail of hand on mridangam: "His gumki ... sang rather than struck."
5. Roll call of artists he played for, fading
6. "It was not volume or display. It was anticipation."
7. Honours ledger, 1990 to 2025
8. "He was a drummer who honoured other drummers."
9. Closing lines from the newsletter, Om Shanti, Rajmohan Krishnan sign-off, logo
