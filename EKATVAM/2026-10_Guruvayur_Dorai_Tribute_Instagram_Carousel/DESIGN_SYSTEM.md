# Design System (this project)
- **Canvas:** 1080 x 1350. Margin 84 px. Kicker top 76 px (29 px, Baskervville SC, tracking .14em). Footer: page number left, wordmark right at 132 px wide.
- **Type:** Baskervville (stand-in for Mrs Eaves). Headlines 400, 92 to 126 px, tracking -.016em. Body 38 to 44 px. Italic for asides and emphasis. SC for kickers, dates, ledger labels.
- **Colour:** Ivory paper `#F0E8DC` (procedural fibre texture, `02_BRAND/textures/`) and Garnet `#4A150E` as the two grounds, alternating. Garnet from the Oxblood family, deeper so type sits calmly. Ink `#3B130D` headlines on ivory, Umber `#5E4E47` body, Ekatvam Red `#942E1C` for emphasis, Blush `#E4BDB4` and Clay `#BE8277` on garnet.
- **Photo:** red-ink duotone, ramp #2A0B07 / #6E1F13 / #BC8271 / #EBDDD0 / #F0E8DC on ivory, deeper ramp for garnet slides. Printed look, grain over everything.
- **Tala ticks:** eight ticks at the foot of each slide, grouped 4 + 2 + 2 as in Adi tala. The current beat is lit. The cycle completes on slide 8.
- **Brushstrokes:** logo strokes 1 to 6 sit on slides 1 to 6, left-bleeding on ivory slides (red) and garnet slides (blush). The complete logo closes slide 8.
- **Rhythm:** photo / paper / paper / photo / dark / paper / paper / dark / paper. Ledger layouts (3, 7, 8) use hairline rules at 28% opacity.
- **Grain:** SVG noise, multiply 10% on Paper, soft-light 50% on dark.
- **Recurring device:** one enlarged brushstroke (slide 2) and the ledger rule.
