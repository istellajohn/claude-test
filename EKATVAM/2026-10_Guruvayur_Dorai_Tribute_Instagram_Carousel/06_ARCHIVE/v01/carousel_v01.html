Today the mridangam lost one of its last links to a golden age.
With him goes a living memory of Carnatic music’s twentieth century, and a way of playing that put the music first and the drummer second.

Ekatvam  ·  In memoriam
Rasikas will tell you what set him apart.
It was not volume or display. It was anticipation.
He seemed to know where the singer was going before the singer arrived, and he was already there to meet them.
