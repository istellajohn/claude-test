# Ekatvam Colour Palette

Sampled from the supplied palette export (`Ekatvam_Palette_Reference.png`), which was taken from the primary logo. Values are screen sRGB; no CMYK/Pantone equivalents supplied yet.

| # | Name (working) | Hex | Role |
|---|----------------|-----|------|
| 1 | Ekatvam Red | `#942E1C` | Logo brushwork. Primary brand colour. |
| 2 | Paper | `#F6F6F6` | Logo ground. Default light background. |
| 3 | Terracotta | `#94483B` | Secondary red, softer than primary. |
| 4 | Umber | `#5E4E47` | Wordmark colour. Default text colour. |
| 5 | Clay | `#BE8277` | Tint, for quiet fields and rules. |
| 6 | Blush Clay | `#E4BDB4` | Lightest tint. Large background fields. |
| 7 | Oxblood | `#822D1D` | Deepest red, for shadow and depth. |

## Notes
- Names are working labels for convenience, not approved brand names.
- Wordmark is Umber on Paper, not black. Do not substitute pure black or pure white as defaults.
- Palette is entirely warm and earthen. Anything cool (blue, green) needs a reason.
- Contrast to check per project: Clay (#BE8277) and Blush Clay on Paper are decorative only, not for text.
