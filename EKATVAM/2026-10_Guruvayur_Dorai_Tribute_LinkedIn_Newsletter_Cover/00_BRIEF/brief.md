# Brief: LinkedIn newsletter cover, "The Drum That Listened"

**Requested:** 3 options for a LinkedIn newsletter cover. Beautiful, elegant, respectful, bespoke, premium.
**Supplied:** newsletter PDF (`supplied_copy/Guruvayur_Dorai_Tribute_Ekatvam.pdf`), brand colours, Ekatvam logo.
**Subject:** In memoriam, Padma Shri Guruvayur Dorai (2 July 1935 – 10 October 2026), mridangist. Author: Rajmohan Krishnan, Founder Trustee, Ekatvam.
**Format:** 1920 x 1080 px (16:9). Held inside a 132 px safe margin so mobile crops do not cut text.
**Imagery:** none supplied. No portrait of Guruvayur Dorai was provided and no AI imagery is used (house rule), so all options are typographic and brand-mark led. A portrait or archival photograph, if the Trust has one with permission, would allow a fourth direction.

**Copy used (all taken from the supplied PDF, nothing added):**
EKATVAM · IN MEMORIAM / The Drum That Listened / Remembering Padma Shri Guruvayur Dorai / 2 July 1935 – 10 October 2026

## Visual ideas
- **A, One Stroke:** One brushstroke from the Ekatvam mark, enlarged, as a single held note over a page of silence.
- **B, Resonance:** The mark's six strokes enlarged until they stop being a logo and become sound decaying in a dark room, tone on tone.
- **C, Span:** The two years as architecture. The dates are the monument and the title is the inscription beneath it.

## Cultural note
Red is Ekatvam's brand colour, not a mourning signal, and it is used with restraint (a single stroke in A, tone-on-tone in B, tiny type in C). Traditional Indian mourning is associated with white and quiet tones, which is why Paper and Umber carry most of each layout. No lamps, lotuses, om symbols or garlands are used.
