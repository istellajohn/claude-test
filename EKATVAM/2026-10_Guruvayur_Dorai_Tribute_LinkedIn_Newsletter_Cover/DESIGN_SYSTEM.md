# Design System (this project)

- **Canvas:** 1920 x 1080. Left margin 132 px. Kicker at top 96 px.
- **Type:** Newsreader only. Title weight 300, optical size 72, tracking -0.016 to -0.018 em, sizes 176 / 164 / 132 px (A / B / C). Subtitle italic 300, 38–40 px. Kicker and dates: 21 px, caps, tracking 0.22–0.28 em, weight 500.
- **Colour:** Umber `#5E4E47` text, Paper `#F6F6F6` ground, Ekatvam Red `#942E1C` accent, Terracotta `#94483B` dates (A), Blush `#E4BDB4` and Clay `#BE8277` tints.
- **Grain:** SVG fractal noise, multiply, 10% (A, C); soft-light 50% (B). Purpose: remove screen flatness so it reads as matte paper.
- **Logo:** full lockup 176 px wide (A), 128 px (C); Paper wordmark 200 px (B). Clear space at least the height of the lowest stroke.
- **Brand mark use:** strokes are enlarged raster crops. Fine at this size, but a vector master is needed before any larger or print use.
