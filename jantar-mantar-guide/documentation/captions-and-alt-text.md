# Captions, forwards and alt text

## Instagram caption

I was at Jantar Mantar 1.0 in July. Communication was a real problem for me that day, and when I tried 112 it didn't help the way it should have.

So for anyone thinking about 2.0 tomorrow, I've put together what I wish I'd had: what's changed since this morning, how to prepare for losing signal, who to call, how to record what happens properly, and what to do if someone you came with goes quiet.

A few honest things. The protest does not have police permission, and restrictions are changing by the hour, so check the latest before you decide anything. The contact numbers are public lines I've checked tonight, but I haven't been able to confirm that any of them will be staffed tomorrow, and none of them has endorsed this. If you're a lawyer, doctor or volunteer who will be there, message me and I'll add you once I've verified you.

Go prepared, or help from where you are. Either way, look after each other.

Save this while you still have signal. Hindi version in the next post.
