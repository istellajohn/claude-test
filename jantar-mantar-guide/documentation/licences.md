## Illustrations and icons

Every illustration and icon was drawn for this project, in code, in `src/illustrations.js`. No stock, AI-generated or third-party artwork is used. The Samrat Yantra drawing is an original stylisation of a public monument.

## Photographs

None are used in the exports. Three reference photographs were shared during briefing: a large crowd with Indian flags, police around a bus with detained protesters, and placards reading "Inquilaab Zindabad" with a copy of the Constitution. Their source and licence are unknown, and at least one appears to be agency news photography. They were not used. To add your own Jantar Mantar 1.0 photographs, place them in `assets/images/` and record each one here.
