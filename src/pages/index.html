A founder’s voice and a company’s credibility are one asset, or two liabilities.

The person

Carries belief, judgement and taste. Travels further, faster and cheaper than any brand channel. Cannot be delegated, and rarely survives a template.

The company

Carries proof, scale and continuity. Outlives any single spokesperson. Sounds hollow when nobody inside it will be specific in public.

We work on either. We are most useful on both at once. The same market reads them together whether or not they were built that way.

Case 01 · Odisha Millets Mission · Odisha → Paris

A tribal grain was repositioned as an international conversation.

Situation
A climate-smart crop with real policy backing and almost no cultural standing outside the state.
Our role
Branding, digital marketing and PR head for the International Year of Millets programme.
Intervention
Move the story off nutrition messaging and onto climate, cuisine and the farmers themselves, especially the women.
Outcome
From tribal plates to UN events and IYOM summits in Paris, features on MasterChef India, and village women starting millet businesses of their own.

Case 02 · 51 Cola · United Kingdom

A cola built for a generation that questions the default.

Category
The most defended shelf in drinks, held at one end by industrial Big Soda and at the other by clinical better-for-you brands.
The problem
Young drinkers read both poles as marketing. A new cola could not win by opposing one of them; it had to stand for something they already believed.
Our role
Category and audience research, brand strategy and positioning, identity and packaging direction, launch strategy, messaging system, campaign creative, PR, creators, digital and retail communication.
The position
Choice, culture, taste and individuality. A brand young consumers participate in rather than get marketed at. Currently scaling.

The Valence test

  1. What does this make someone feel in the first second?
  2. What survives once every detail is forgotten?
  3. What of that turns into attraction, trust or conviction?

We answer these three before anything is designed, written, filmed or booked.

03Engagement

Decode before deciding. Decide before making anything.

Five stages. No discovery theatre, no thirty-page audit nobody reads.

  1. 01

    Decode

    How you are read today, by whom, and the gap between intent and interpretation.

  2. 02

    Position

    The claim, the refusals, the hierarchy. One page the whole company can repeat.

  3. 03

    Express

    Identity, language, founder voice, film and design: the position made physical.

  4. 04

    Distribute

    Press, digital, creators, talent, search. Landing it where the decision is made.

  5. 05

    Compound

    Quarterly recalibration so authority accumulates instead of resetting.

Stage one is paid, standalone and useful on its own. Most relationships begin there and continue by choice, not by contract length.
