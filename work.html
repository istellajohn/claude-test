A few of the worlds we have worked inside.

Our work takes different forms in images, language, identity and campaigns. Each project holds a different set of people and decisions; these pages make room for the context as well as the finished work.

Project 01 · Odisha Millets Mission · Odisha → Paris

A tribal grain was repositioned as an international conversation.

The brief
A climate-smart crop with real policy backing and almost no cultural standing outside the state.
Our role
Branding, digital marketing and PR head for the International Year of Millets programme.
Creative decisions
Move the story off nutrition messaging and onto climate, cuisine and the farmers themselves, especially the women.
Outcome
From tribal plates to UN events and IYOM summits in Paris, features on MasterChef India, and village women starting millet businesses of their own.
United KingdomConsumer · launch

Project 02 · 51 Cola · United Kingdom

A cola built for a generation that questions the default.

Project
The most defended shelf in drinks, held at one end by industrial Big Soda and at the other by clinical better-for-you brands.
The brief
Young drinkers read both poles as marketing. A new cola could not win by opposing one of them; it had to stand for something they already believed.
Our role
Project and audience research, brand strategy and positioning, identity and packaging direction, launch strategy, messaging system, campaign creative, PR, creators, digital and retail communication.
Creative decisions
Choice, culture, taste and individuality. A brand young consumers participate in rather than get marketed at. Currently scaling.
AuthorityPrivate capital

Project 03 · Authority · Rajmohan Krishnan, Entrust Family Office

In a business built entirely on trust, the founder’s judgement is the product.

Advising families whose decisions run across generations, the firm’s credibility was real and almost entirely private. Every new relationship started from zero and was carried by referral alone.

Creative decisions
What he stands for, and what he will refuse to advise.
What we made
Ghostwritten in his own language, at a cadence he can sustain.
Outcome
Positions that accumulate into authority instead of resetting each quarter.
