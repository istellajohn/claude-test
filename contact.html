Contact

Tell us what has taken up room in your head.

You might have a project ready to begin, or an idea whose form is still taking shape. Tell us what you know about it and what you would like help with. A few honest lines give us somewhere to start.

You can also write directly to buzz@vlnc.in.

Tell us about the idea, what matters to you about it and where you would like us to come in.

An approximate range helps us understand what we can plan for.

Questions before getting in touch

Do I need a finished brief?

You can begin with an idea, a problem or a piece of work that feels unresolved. Tell us the part you can explain, and we can use the first conversation to understand what the project needs.

Can I bring you one piece of work?

Yes. You can approach us for a particular photograph, piece of writing, identity or campaign. We agree on its purpose, direction and scope before beginning.

Can you work with our existing team?

We can discuss a role alongside your team, production partners or current collaborators. We agree on who is responsible for what and how decisions will be made, so the relationship supports the work.

How do you price the work?

Fees depend on the scope, production requirements and level of involvement. Share the project and any budget you have in mind, and we can discuss what an appropriate engagement would look like.

How do we begin?

Send a note through the form or write to buzz@vlnc.in. Include a little about the project, where you would like help and any dates we should know about.
