# Brief: Jantar Mantar, 10 October 2026

## The ask
A short, fast, heavily cut video from still photographs of the day at Jantar Mantar. Editorial, grunge, cinematic, with the pressure of a revolution film.

## Date check (resolved)
The request said 10 October **2025**. A search found no Jantar Mantar event on that date; the event on 10 October **2026** is the Cockroach Janta Party protest. Confirmed with the client: 2026.

## Material

## Reported but NOT verified (do not caption)
- Figures for detentions (around 1,000 was reported in a search snippet) and named people detained. Not confirmed from a primary or fetchable source.
- Anything about what happened on the day, until a source we can open says so.

## Documentary rules for this edit
- Photos stay in order of capture (EXIF time) where available. No reshuffling for drama.
- A repeated photo is shown only as a different crop of the same frame, and the timeline says so.
- Captions carry attributed facts only, never allegations as findings. No invented dialogue, no generated imagery.
- Faces of detained or vulnerable people: decide before publishing. Consider blurring or omitting.
- Style lives in grade, rhythm, type and sound. It never alters what a photograph shows.

## Message (client, 10 Oct 2026)
The film is not about Jantar Mantar alone. It is about the whole country standing together and fighting. This is the maker's message and is carried as type cards in the maker's voice ("We stand together." / "Together."). It is not a caption: the 16 photographs show Delhi only, and nothing on screen claims where else protests happened.
