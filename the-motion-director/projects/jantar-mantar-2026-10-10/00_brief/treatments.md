# Three treatments (photo montage)

All three use the original score and the `grunge` looks. Run `./tmd rough-cut jantar-mantar-2026-10-10 --music <analysis json>` to generate them.

**A. The Impact Cut** (`grunge_mono`, ~132 BPM). Slow start, then 0.2 to 0.45 s photographs through the drop, white flash only on downbeats, a single held photograph through the break and the silence, a final hit.

**B. The Cinematic Cut** (`grunge_ember`). 1.5 to 3 s holds, slow push-ins, the ember-red shadows. The silence does the work. Fewer cuts, more weight.

**C. The Experimental Editorial Cut** (`grunge_mono`). Irregular rhythm, detail crops of the same photograph (top, bottom, mirrored) to build a graphic, collage-like pulse. Needs the clearest photos.

Pick the one the photographs support. With fewer than about 8 distinct photos, A will repeat frames; prefer B or C-with-crops, or ask for more material.
