# Workflow (per project)

1. **Brief.** `00_brief/brief.md`. One essential question at a time, only if something truly blocks the work.
2. **Ingest.** Inventory, shot log, proxies. Read the warnings: HDR sources and variable frame rate are reported, not silently fixed.
3. **Story discovery.** Watch the footage. The inventory points at hero candidates, human moments, motion and sound opportunities and technical problems; a person decides what the film is about.
4. **Music.** Research candidates on Spotify and elsewhere; add only real, checked tracks to the shortlist; record Instagram availability and licence status with evidence. Analyse a licensed or guide file for beats and structure.
5. **Treatment.** Three genuinely different directions (A impact, B cinematic, C experimental editorial). `rough-cut` seeds all three; choose the one the material supports and record it in `project.json`.
6. **Rough → fine cut.** Storyboard inspector and timeline JSON. Re-render previews often; the preview is half size and includes the guide track.
7. **Sound, motion, colour.** J/L bridges, designed sound layers, ducking, overlays, captions, grade.
8. **Review.** Timeline Preview with frame stepping and the Reels safe-area guide.
9. **Export.** `./tmd export <project> <timeline> [--all]`. Read every QC warning.
10. **Archive.** Commit timelines, captions, visual system, shortlist, licences and reports. Media stays out of Git unless you set up private LFS.
