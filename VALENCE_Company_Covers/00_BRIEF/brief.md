# Brief: VALENCE company covers v01

**Assignment (Stella, 2026-10-09):** company cover for LinkedIn, Facebook and YouTube. Find accurate sizes, design them to the VALENCE standard, show before finalising. Final size 4K or 2K.

**Supplied:** `02_BRAND/guidelines/VALENCE_Brand_Identity_System.pdf` (20 pp) and six transparent PNG logos (stacked VLNC, wide VLNC, Valence signature; black and white of each).

**Standing instruction:** the identity is still evolving ("we modify and do not stay still"). Every cover is built so the logo can be swapped from one folder and the whole set rebuilt (`04_WORKING/source_files/build_covers.py`).

**Fonts:** free, commercially licensed only. Deck fonts (Sohne Breit, Neue Haas Grotesk, Canela) are paid, so licensed substitutes are used. See `ASSET_REGISTER.md`.

**Copy used (all taken from the deck, none invented):** "Find the signal. Remove the noise." (p4), "We shape how brands are felt, read and remembered." (p18), "Strategy / Story / Growth / Culture", "Branding & Marketing Agency", and the four charges Attraction, Trust, Memory, Conviction (p3).
