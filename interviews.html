Conversations

in the particulars

People become interesting in the particulars. We are interested in the habit a person cannot quite account for, the thing they changed their mind about or the work they still find difficult. Our written conversations move across creative and business life, following the details that make a person’s way of seeing their own.

    We could not find a story matching that search. Try a name, subject or a shorter phrase.

    Asked in every conversation

    We ask these questions because the answers belong to the person.

    1. 01

      What have you learned to see that you once walked past?

    2. 02

      What part of your work would surprise the person you were when you began?

    3. 03

      What are you still doing simply because you love it?
