Interviews

in conversation

Long conversations with people whose substance runs ahead of how they are seen: cinematographers, founders, scientists, makers. Every one ends with the same three questions.

8 shown

    The ritual

    Three questions, last, every time

    1. 01

      What does this make someone feel in the first second?

    2. 02

      What survives once every detail is forgotten?

    3. 03

      What of that turns into attraction, trust or conviction?
